# Notice

This repository contains an independently written synthesis of research-writing conventions observed in selected publicly available AAAI papers. It is intended as a writing aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authored by the researchers, institutions, conference, or publishers associated with those papers.

The repository does not contain the source papers or substantial verbatim excerpts from them. Paper titles are listed only to identify the public works used as the methodological basis of the synthesis. Copyright in the original papers remains with their respective rights holders.
